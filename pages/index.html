Easel

A calm, offline gallery for your photos on GNOME.

Install
  • Install with GNOME Software Opens your app store; updates arrive like any other app

Easel shows the photos already on your disk, where they are. No importing, no cloud, no account — it reads your folders in place and never goes online, not even for the map.

Browse everything at once or by folder, album, person or place, and make quick edits that are saved as a new copy, so the original stays exactly as it was.

Questions

Which files can Easel show?

JPEG, PNG, WebP, HEIC/HEIF, AVIF, GIF and TIFF photos, plus MP4, MOV, MKV and WebM videos, with thumbnails for both.

Do I have to import or move my photos?

No. Easel reads your folders in place and watches them for new photos. It never moves, renames or copies anything into a library of its own.

Does Easel change my photos?

No. Edits, crops, rotations and filters are saved as a new copy that keeps the original's date and other EXIF data. Albums, favourites and names are kept in Easel's own folder. The only change it makes on disk is moving a photo to the Trash when you ask it to.

How does the map work without going online?

Easel ships its own world map, built from OpenStreetMap data, and places each photo by the GPS location in its metadata. No map tiles are ever downloaded — Easel has no network access at all.

Why does GNOME Software list broad permissions?

Easel can read your home folder so it can show photos wherever you keep them, and network shares or phones mounted in Files. It also reads your system's icon theme so its buttons match your desktop. All of this is read-only, and Easel has no network access to upload anything. Saving an edited copy or moving a photo to the Trash goes through your desktop's own file dialog and Trash, and only when you ask.

Why isn't Easel on Flathub?

Flathub doesn't accept new apps made with AI assistance, and Easel was built with help from Claude. It's published from its own signed repository instead, and updates arrive through GNOME Software or flatpak update like any other app.

How do I install or remove it from a terminal?

To install:

flatpak remote-add --user --if-not-exists easel \
    https://drvonmiau.github.io/easel/index.flatpakrepo
flatpak install --user easel io.github.drvonmiau.Easel

To remove Easel and its repository:

flatpak uninstall --user io.github.drvonmiau.Easel
flatpak remote-delete --user easel
